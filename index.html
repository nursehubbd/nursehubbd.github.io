📋 Vital Signs, Electrolytes & ABG

Adult HR, RR & BP: HR: 60–100 | RR: 12–20 | BP: <120/80
Newborn Vitals: HR: 120–160 bpm | RR: 30–60 /min
Serum Na⁺, K⁺ & Ca²⁺: Na⁺: 135–145 | K⁺: 3.5–5.0 | Ca²⁺: 8.5–10.5
Normal ABG Values: pH: 7.35–7.45 | PaCO₂: 35–45 | HCO₃⁻: 22–26
Hb, Platelets & WBC: Hb: 12–17.5 | Plt: 1.5–4.5L | WBC: 4.5–11k
Fasting Glucose & Cr: FBS: 3.9–5.5 mmol/L | Cr: 0.6–1.2 mg/dL
Therapeutic Levels: Lithium: 0.6–1.2 mEq/L | Digoxin: 0.5–2.0 ng/mL

🧪 Essential Clinical Antidotes

Paracetamol: N-Acetylcysteine (NAC)
Opioids (Morphine): Naloxone (Narcan)
Heparin Overdose: Protamine Sulfate
Warfarin: Vitamin K₁ (Phytonadione)
MgSO₄ Toxicity: 10% Calcium Gluconate
Benzodiazepines: Flumazenil
OPC Poisoning: Atropine + Pralidoxime
